About GForg3Labs

GForg3Labs, short for Ghost Forge Labs, was founded by Mohammad Omar Hayat to build the apps he wanted for his own family: a Quran reader that keeps the translation beside the Arabic, prayer times that just work, and a phone that elderly parents can use without help.

We use AI to design, write and test our apps faster, and we keep a person in charge of every decision. We work across Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, and design every app for Urdu, Arabic and English speakers.

  • Easy for every ageLarge controls, clear text and support for the phone's own font size, so parents and grandparents can use our apps too.
  • Respect for your privacyWe collect as little as possible and never sell personal data.
  • Works when the internet doesn'tCore features keep working offline once content is downloaded.
  • Made for our regionUrdu, Arabic and English from the start, not added later.

Privacy policy

This policy covers all apps published by GForg3Labs (Ghost Forge Labs), including Quran Al-Kareem and Salah: Prayer Times & Qibla.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Information we collect

Our apps are designed to work without an account. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email inside the apps.

  • Location (Salah): used on your device to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. It is not sent to our servers or stored by us.
  • Settings, bookmarks and downloads: kept on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Downloaded content: Quran text, translations, fonts and recitations are downloaded from public sources. These services may see your device's IP address, as with any download.
  • Feedback: if you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

How we use information

Only to provide the app's features and to answer your messages. We do not show ads, sell personal data or share it with advertisers.

Crash reports and app statistics

Google Play and Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and download statistics. These do not identify you.

Children

Our apps are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time and enter your city by hand instead. You can ask us to delete any email you have sent us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Apps that add new features using personal data will explain them in the app first.
